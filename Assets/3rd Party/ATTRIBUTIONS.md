## FlappyBirdAssets-Megacrash

- **Folder:** `Assets/3rd Party/FlappyBirdAssets-Megacrash/`
- **Author:** Megacrash
- **Version:** 1.6
- **License:** Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal (CC0) — public domain dedication,
  free for commercial and non-commercial use, no attribution required. Credited here as good practice.
- **Colour palette:** Endesga64
- **Contents used:** pipe and ground tilesets (`Tiles/Style 1`), backgrounds (`Background/`)
- **Note:** the pack's bird sprites (`Player/`) and pipe styles 2-5 were removed from this project
  as unused. Only the retained files are listed above.
- **Original licence text:** preserved verbatim in `FlappyBirdAssets-Megacrash/readme.txt`

The pack is original artwork inspired by the Flappy Bird genre. It does not contain assets
extracted from the original game.
